# Microbial SAE interpretation

Explain the whole activation pattern, not only its highest-activating taxon. Begin
with population coverage and inspect taxonomy, environment, abundance, community
context and study concentration. Read selectively from the evidence index.

Keep P(group | active), P(active | group), enrichment over background and activation
magnitude distinct. Events, communities and studies have different denominators.
Top examples illustrate a pattern; their frequencies do not estimate prevalence.

Use the exported five-bin labels and transformation definitions. A lower nonzero
abundance bin is not absence. If thresholds are unavailable, describe relative bins
without inventing absolute abundance cutoffs. Consider the focal OTU's abundance
and community diversity/composition separately.

For a proposed biological explanation, inspect an informative contrasting case or
matched control when available. Same taxon across communities, similar abundance
across taxa and replication across studies answer different questions. A precomputed
matched comparison is not a new intervention or an independently confirmed result.

There is no preferred feature class. A bounded descriptive association can be a
good explanation; neither a mechanistic story nor an unknown label is required.
Distinguish minority subpatterns from the feature's dominant coverage. Preserve
important contradictions and state one useful discriminating follow-up when needed.
Lead with the result; avoid repetitive generic caveats. Write all reports in English.
